BEFORE YOU JOIN

What do I get every single day?
Every trading day: a pre-market outlook with key levels and bias before the session opens, live real-time signals as I place trades, and a post-session trade recap breaking down every entry and exit. On top of that, a full weekly P&L review every Friday.
What instrument do you trade?
MNQ — Micro Nasdaq futures. All signals and sessions are MNQ-specific. The mentorship course covers the full MNQ framework including risk management, setup criteria, and execution.
Are your results verified?
Yes. Every stat on this page is pulled from Tradesyncer — a third-party trading journal connected directly to the live account. Winning and losing trades included. The full data is also publicly available in the Instagram highlights at @wardynq.
What timezone are the sessions and signals?
GMT. UK-based, so everything is timed around the London open (8am GMT) and the US open (1:30pm GMT) — the two highest-probability sessions on MNQ. No 3am alerts.
What's included in the mentorship course?
The full course covers MNQ market structure, the exact setup criteria used in every trade, risk management framework, trading psychology and discipline, and execution process from pre-market to close. It's the complete system — not isolated modules.
What's the difference between Signal Group and 1:1 Mentorship?
The Club Signals (£50/mo) gives you everything — daily outlook, live sessions, signals, recaps, course, and community. The Club 1:1 Mentorship (£150/mo) adds direct weekly coaching with Max, your own trades reviewed personally, a plan built around your specific situation, and direct WhatsApp access. Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no lock-ins. Cancel directly through Whop in one click. The Signal Group is month-to-month. 1:1 spots are reviewed monthly.
Is this suitable for beginners?
The course starts from the foundations, so beginners can follow. That said, if you're completely new to futures, DM on Instagram first and I'll tell you honestly whether now is the right time or if there's groundwork to lay first.
