Before you join. Questions.

What do I get every single day?

Every trading day: a pre-market outlook with key levels and bias before the session opens, live trade breakdowns as I place trades, and a post-session trade recap breaking down every entry and exit. On top of that, a full weekly P&L review every Friday.

What instrument do you trade?

The Nasdaq 100 — traded through MNQ, the Micro E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures contract. All sessions and course content are Nasdaq 100-specific. The mentorship course covers the full Nasdaq 100 framework including risk management, setup criteria, and execution.

Are your results verified?

Yes. Every live-trading stat on this page is pulled from Tradesyncer — a third-party trading journal connected directly to the live account. Winning and losing trades included. The full data is also publicly available in the Instagram highlights at @theclub.trading.

What timezone are the sessions?

UK time. UK-based, so everything is timed around the London open (8am UK time) and the US open (2:30pm UK time for most of the year) — the two highest-probability sessions on the Nasdaq 100. No 3am alerts.

What's included in the mentorship course?

The full course covers Nasdaq 100 market structure, the exact setup criteria used in every trade, risk management framework, trading psychology and discipline, and execution process from pre-market to close. It's the complete system — not isolated modules.

What's the difference between Membership and 1:1 Mentorship?

The Club Membership (£50/mo) gives you everything — daily outlook, live sessions, trade breakdowns, recaps, course, and community. The Club 1:1 Mentorship (£150/mo) adds direct weekly coaching with Max, your own trades reviewed personally, a plan built around your specific situation, and direct WhatsApp access. Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-ins. Cancel directly through Whop in one click. Membership is month-to-month. 1:1 spots are reviewed monthly.

Is this suitable for beginners?

The course starts from the foundations, so beginners can follow. That said, if you're completely new to futures, DM on Instagram first and I'll tell you honestly whether now is the right time or if there's groundwork to lay first.
