Myth vs reality.

The same things we talk about on Instagram, and the first things we'll be honest about on the call.

Myth

Trading is basically gambling.

Reality

It's a skill built on rules, risk management and repetition, and it takes time to learn.

Myth

Good traders don't lose.

Reality

Losses are part of trading. What matters is controlling them and staying disciplined.

Myth

You'll be quitting your job in a month.

Reality

There's no shortcut. Patience and consistent practice come first.
